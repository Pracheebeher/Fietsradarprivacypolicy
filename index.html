Fietsradar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026
Publisher: Prachee Behera (individual developer)
Contact: app.support.2019@gmail.com

Fietsradar is a cycling weather app for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can control it. Fietsradar does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

Data we use on your device

  • Location (precise, when you allow it) — to show weather, rain radar, and ride conditions near you or along a route you check.
  • Home / work / other places you save — stored on device to score your commute and power the widget.
  • Settings — ride length, speed, alert preferences, theme, and language, stored on device.
  • Cached weather — short-lived forecast cache so the app can reopen faster.
  • Local notifications — optional rain, gust, and departure reminders scheduled on your device only (no push server).
  • Purchases — if you subscribe to Premium, Apple processes the payment. Fietsradar does not receive your card details.

Data sent off your device

When you use weather, search, maps, radar, or national alerts, coordinates or place queries may be sent over HTTPS to:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts
  • Apple MapKit / geocoding — maps, place search, and reverse geocoding
  • RainViewer — live rain radar tiles
  • Meteoalarm / KNMI (via Meteoalarm feed) — Netherlands weather warnings
  • Apple — App Store / StoreKit for Premium subscriptions (when you buy or restore)

Those providers process requests under their own terms. Fietsradar does not run ads, does not use an advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Settings and caches stay on your iPhone until you change them, clear app data, or uninstall Fietsradar. Uninstalling removes app-stored data from the device. There is no Fietsradar cloud account to delete. Manage or cancel a Premium subscription in your Apple ID subscriptions.

Children

Fietsradar is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
